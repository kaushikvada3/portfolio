003 roles

  • Perform static timing analysis on die-to-die interconnects in a next-generation AI accelerator SoC with RISC-V cores, using Synopsys PrimeTime with post-route netlists, SPEF parasitics, UPF power intent, and SDC constraints.
  • Analyze multi-voltage timing paths through level shifters connecting a dynamic-voltage SoC domain to a fixed-voltage PHY domain, evaluating voltage-dependent timing behavior.
  • Extract corner-specific parasitics with Synopsys StarRC on Fusion Compiler databases, and configure automated RTL-to-GDS flows from synthesis through route optimization.
  • Investigate AI/ML-based corner selection targeting a reduction from ~46 PVT corners to 10, evaluating derating and guard-banding strategies that preserve timing coverage and shorten signoff turnaround.

PrimeTime · StarRC · Fusion Compiler · SPEF · UPF · SDC

  • Delivered a tape-out candidate for an active Arm AGI-CPU SoC block — running the Cadence Innovus flow from floorplanning through placement, CTS, routing, and post-route optimization, with activity-driven power optimization and two-pass placement. The implementation was advanced for downstream STA and timing evaluation.
  • Compared multiple FSDB activity profiles to guide selection on power and timing; activity-driven power optimization yielded 18.4–19.5% lower reported power versus baselines under matched workloads.
  • Co-developed a two-pass placement flow with Cadence engineers, reducing estimated power by 5.86% and cell area by 2.45%.
  • Reduced measured mean RX data-path delay by 38.5%, using Codex-assisted statistical modeling of prior runs to guide setup-margin tuning — deliberately tightening constraints on positive-slack I/O paths to trigger optimization toward full-chip timing requirements.
  • Enabled reusable STA and PrimeTime PX workflows: analysis scripts that compare implementation results, audit generated Tcl/YAML against applied constraints, and support international engineering teams.

Innovus · PrimeTime PX · FSDB activity · Tcl / YAML
